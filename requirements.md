# Dhivix – AI for Bharat

## Problem Statement
Citizens, students, entrepreneurs, and MSMEs face frequent rejections and delays while accessing
banking and government services due to document mismatches, manual workflows, and limited digital access.

## Target Users
- Citizens / Account Holders
- Students
- Entrepreneurs
- MSMEs
- Branch / Service Staff

## Key Requirements
- Secure document upload and storage
- AI-based data extraction from documents
- Detection of mismatches (Name, DOB, Aadhaar–PAN)
- Auto-filled application forms
- Human verification before final submission
- Application status tracking

## Expected Impact
Reduced rejections, faster processing, and inclusive access to services.
