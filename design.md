# System Design

## Overview
Dhivix follows a hybrid AI-assisted, human-verified workflow designed for real-world service delivery in India.

## Workflow
1. User or trained staff uploads documents
2. AI extracts data using OCR and NLP
3. AI detects mismatches across records
4. AI auto-fills official forms
5. Human verifies the information
6. Final submission is completed
7. User tracks status

## AI Components
- OCR for document reading
- NLP for field extraction
- Rule-based validation

## Human Role
- Assisted onboarding
- Compliance verification
- Final approval

## Privacy & Ethics
- Masked Aadhaar
- Consent-based document reuse
- Audit trail maintained
